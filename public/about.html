About ChadAds

List it. Sell it. Keep it local.

ChadAds is a local classifieds website built to be fast, clean and easy to use. People list the things they want to sell, and buyers nearby find them by choosing a city and a distance.

We serve the Capital District, Albany, Saratoga, Troy, the Adirondacks, the Hudson Valley and Upstate New York.

What you can find

  • Cars, trucks, motorcycles, ATVs, campers and trailers
  • Farm equipment and heavy machinery
  • Tools, lawn and garden equipment, and sports gear

How it works

  • Browse by category, search by keyword, and filter by distance from your city.
  • List your item for free with up to five photos, and add a YouTube or Vimeo video link if you have one.
  • Save the ads you like to your account and come back to them.
  • Share any ad with a link to Facebook, X, WhatsApp, email or a message.

Your privacy

Photos you upload have their hidden location data removed before they are stored. Read more in our Privacy Policy.

Stay safe

  • Meet in a public place, in daylight, if you can.
  • Inspect the item before you pay, and be careful with large payments.
  • Never share passwords or one-time codes with anyone.
  • If a deal sounds too good to be true, it probably is.
