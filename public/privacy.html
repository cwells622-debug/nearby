Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what ChadAds collects and why. We keep it to what the site needs to work.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, a display name, and your password (stored in scrambled form, never in plain text).
  • What you post: ad titles, descriptions, prices, the city you choose, links, photos and videos. Ads are public.
  • What you save: the ads you mark as saved. These are private to your account.
  • Technical information: like your IP address and browser type, which our hosting and database services record in their normal logs.

Photos and videos

Photos you upload have hidden details such as GPS location removed in your browser before they are stored. For video clips we blank GPS positions stored in the file, but other hidden details, such as the phone model or date, may remain. If a detail matters to you, remove it before uploading.

Location

You choose a city for each ad and to search nearby. If you press "Use my location", your browser asks permission and the position is used on your device to pick the closest city. It is not stored.

How we use it

  • To run the site: show ads, keep you signed in, and let you save and manage your ads.
  • To keep the site safe, for example to prevent spam and remove ads that break the rules.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and similar storage

Your browser keeps a sign-in session so you stay logged in, and the site remembers a few display settings. We do not use advertising cookies. If you play a YouTube or Vimeo video, that service may set its own cookies.

Who handles the data

ChadAds uses Supabase to store accounts, ads and files, and Netlify to host the website. They process data for us under their own privacy terms.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your ads at any time from the site.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data.

Children

ChadAds is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
