Tips for an ad that sells

  • Take photos in daylight. Show the front, back and sides, the interior, the odometer or hour meter, and anything that needs work.
  • Be specific. Year, make, model, mileage or hours, condition, and why you are selling. Honest ads get more replies.
  • Price it fairly. Look at similar ads, and say if the price is firm or open to offers.
  • Reply quickly. Buyers move on fast.

Questions

Does it cost anything to list?

No. Listing your item on ChadAds is free.

Can I edit or delete my ad?

Yes, at any time. Open your ad and choose Edit this ad or Delete this ad.

Will buyers see my email address?

No. Buyers contact you through private messages inside ChadAds.

Does ChadAds handle payment?

No. You and the buyer agree on price, payment and pickup directly. Meet in a public place and inspect the item before any money changes hands. See our safety tips.

How will I know someone messaged me?

A Messages button with an unread count appears at the top of ChadAds when you are logged in. Email alerts are coming.
